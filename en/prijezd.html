Arrival & parking

Your step-by-step guide to arrival, parking and entering the cottage.

1. Arrival 2. Parking 3. Approach and house entrance

Arrival

Address: Blansko 564, Jablonec nad Jizerou

Parking

Important points:

  • Point 1: Entrance to the cottage
  • Point 2: 5 parking spaces by the garages, approximately 50 m from the house.
  • Point 3: A short stop alongside the road for loading and unloading.
  • Point 4: Any additional parking is subject to prior arrangement.

Loading/unloading tip: To load or unload luggage, stop briefly alongside the road by the house (point 3). Face towards the garages and keep the road clear.

Approach and house entrance

How to get inside:

  1. Look for the brown-and-white house and the guest entrance sign.
  2. We will send you the key box code before arrival.
  3. The key is stored in a code-protected key box.
