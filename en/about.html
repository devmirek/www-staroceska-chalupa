About us

Today the cottage is not only a home, but also a memory of times long past, when every piece of wood, every building and every object had its meaning and story.

How it all began

The cottage is about 160 years old. Its origins are linked to the 1866–1872 building of the road from Poniklá along the Jizera to Nový Svět (part of Harrachov) and above all of its branch from Jablonec nad Jizerou to Sklenařice.

According to our ancestors and former neighbours, the cottage stands partly on fill from building the branch road and partly on rock.

Municipal boundaries then usually followed watercourses, so Blansko on the right bank of the Jizera, where the cottage stands, belonged to Sklenařice. Today it is part of Jablonec nad Jizerou.

Life in the cottage

The cottage was built by the family our great-grandfather married into – as was usual then, with tiny windows to keep the heat in and with low ceilings and door frames. He then kept improving it.

Everyone lived together in the living kitchen, later our great-grandparents with their four sons. It also held a loom and a wooden workbench on which one of the sons made wooden skis. For several years the family of one son lived here too, and the tiny stone pantry opposite the kitchen was turned into a room for them.

The workbench is now in the common room, along with remnants of old dishes and porcelain. There or in the hallways you will also find tools, skis, sledges and many other things. Some family items, however, come from a cottage in Česká Skalice that no longer exists, where our other great-grandmother lived.

The barn through the years

In 1930 our great-grandfather, with the help of his sons, extended the cottage with a two-storey barn. Until then, the dark log wall with a window at today's upstairs room was the cottage's outer wall.

The decaying barn was partly demolished in 2000 and then rebuilt. We wanted to keep its original look of brick pillars with boards in between, but the building authority disagreed.

The barn became guest accommodation with the original beams, timber and all the antique furniture.
