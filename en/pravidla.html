House rules

We are glad you are here and we hope you enjoy your stay

Do not hesitate to contact us anytime if needed: +420 724 825 447

Entry & WiFi

  • During your stay, always keep the key in the lock box with the code you received before arrival
  • Lock from inside using the key on the hook and do not leave it in the lock
  • When leaving and at night, always lock both entrances to the house
  • WiFi password (QR code) can be found on signs in the entrance hall and dining room
  • We ask all guests to register in the guest book

Water conservation

  • The well is not bottomless – thank you for conserving water
  • If hot water has not been used for a while, let it run before use
  • Drinking water runs in the trough outside

Waste & recycling

  • We sort waste – there are bins for plastic, glass, paper, metals, fats and oils in the kitchen
  • The rubbish bins are at the main entrance near the ski room and wood storage
  • Bio-waste – compost behind the house (raw plant material only)

Toilet

  • Please flush only toilet paper down the toilet
  • Everything else (wet wipes, hygiene products, nappies, hair, cotton buds) please dispose of in the waste bin

Pets

  • Dogs are welcome by prior arrangement
  • Please clean up after them thoroughly in the garden and around the house

Fire & barbecue

  • A portable fire pit, grill and roasting forks are available – please clean after use
  • Place the fire pit only on stones in front of the pergola and use charcoal only
  • Always extinguish the fire thoroughly, be careful in wind and do not scorch the pergola roof
  • The fireplace in the common room can be used with wood

Check-out

  • Please vacate rooms by 10:00 AM (unless agreed otherwise)
  • New guests arrive from 16:00
  • Strip bed linen and sheets
  • Wash dishes and empty the dishwasher
  • Do not leave food in the kitchen
  • Leave room keys in the lock
  • Lock up behind you (including entrance and garden), return key to the lock box
  • Something broken? Let us know – we'll work it out

We wish everyone a pleasant stay!
