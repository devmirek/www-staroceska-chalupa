House rules

Welcome. Here is everything you need for a comfortable stay and a smooth departure.

Need help? Call us anytime: +420 724 825 447

Entry & WiFi Check-out Arrival & parking

Entry & WiFi

  • Keep the key in the key box throughout your stay. Use the code you received before arrival.
  • Lock the door from inside using the key on the hook; do not leave it in the lock afterwards.
  • Lock both entrances whenever you leave and at night.
  • The Wi-Fi password and QR code are on the signs in the entrance hall and dining room.
  • Please make sure every guest signs the accommodation register.

Water conservation

  • The well has a limited supply. Thank you for conserving water.
  • If the hot water has not been used for a while, let it run briefly before use.
  • Drinking water runs in the trough outside

Waste & recycling

  • In the kitchen, separate plastic, glass, paper, metals, fats and oils into their respective containers.
  • The bins are by the ski room and wood store, accessible from the main entrance.
  • Only raw plant scraps belong on the compost heap behind the house.

Toilet

  • Please flush toilet paper only.
  • Put everything else, including wet wipes, sanitary products, nappies, hair and cotton buds, in the bin.

Pets

  • Dogs are welcome by prior arrangement.
  • Please clean up thoroughly after your dogs in the garden and around the house.

Fire & barbecue

  • A portable fire pit, barbecue and roasting forks are available. Please clean them after use.
  • Place the fire pit only on the stones in front of the pergola. Use charcoal only.
  • Always extinguish the fire completely. Be careful in windy weather and protect the pergola roof from heat.
  • You may burn wood in the stove in the common room.

Check-out

New guests arrive from 16:00. Please vacate your rooms by 10:00 on departure, unless agreed otherwise.

  • Strip covers, pillowcases and sheets.
  • Wash the dishes and empty the dishwasher.
  • Remove all your food from the kitchen.
  • Leave the room keys in their locks.
  • Lock the house and garden entrance, then return the key to the key box.
  • If something has broken, please let us know. We will work out a solution together.

Thank you, and enjoy your stay!
