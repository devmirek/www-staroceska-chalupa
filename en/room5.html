Largest room in the cottage, mezzanine with 2+3 beds on the upper floor

In our largest room you will feel truly royal. The room is dominated by wooden stairs to the upper sleeping area, where children love to settle in. On the lower level there is a double bed, a table with benches, and a wardrobe for your belongings. The views from the windows face the colourful garden and the pergola with seating under the green roof.
